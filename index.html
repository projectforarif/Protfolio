About

Five years in, I still start every project the same way: with the problem, not the palette.

I'm a brand design specialist, working with founders, agencies and product teams to build identity systems that don't fall apart the moment they leave a pitch deck — logo, type, color, motion, and the guidelines that keep it all consistent once the studio isn't in the room.

Recent work spans food & beverage, wellness, and sport. If it involves a strong point of view and a system that has to scale, I'm interested.
